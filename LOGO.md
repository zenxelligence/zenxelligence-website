# Zen xElligence — Logo brief

Use this to design the mark. Site tokens stay in `DESIGN.md`. Do not invent a second palette.

There is **no finished logo yet**. The site uses a temporary lockup: accent `Zx` + `ZEN xELLIGENCE`. Replace that when the real files land.

---

## Job of the logo

One mark that can sit on a dark site, a light letterhead, an app icon, and a chip package without looking like a SaaS gradient or a consulting crest.

It must read as a **product + services studio** that ships:

1. Web apps & APIs  
2. Android/iOS Apps  
3. AI Agent Automation  
4. IoT  
5. VLSI  

Not five icons glued together. One studio.

## Color (lock these)

Logo color is the site color. No extra brand green, no blue, no gold.

| Role | Name | Hex | RGB | CMYK (print start) | Use on the mark |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Accent | Signal | `#3ddc84` | `61, 220, 132` | `62, 0, 55, 0` | Primary mark, `Zx`, live states |
| Foreground | Paper | `#f2f2ef` | `242, 242, 239` | `4, 3, 5, 5` | Wordmark on dark |
| Background | Void | `#0b0c0e` | `11, 12, 14` | `70, 60, 50, 80` | Default field |
| Raised | Plate | `#101114` | `16, 17, 20` | `65, 55, 48, 75` | Dark panels, favicon tile |
| Muted | Ash | `#8a8d91` | `138, 141, 145` | `40, 30, 28, 20` | Tagline only — not the mark |
| Border | Rule | `#1e2023` | `30, 32, 35` | `55, 45, 40, 70` | Boxes, not fills |

Pantone: pick the closest **on coated** when print starts. Do not guess a PMS in the SVG.

### Allowed colorways

1. **Primary (dark)** — accent mark + paper wordmark on `#0b0c0e`  
2. **Inverse (light)** — accent mark + `#0b0c0e` wordmark on paper / white  
3. **Mono dark** — full mark in `#f2f2ef` on `#0b0c0e` (emboss, press, one-color)  
4. **Mono light** — full mark in `#0b0c0e` on paper  
5. **Accent only** — full mark in `#3ddc84` on void or paper (icon, favicon, social)

### Not allowed

- White `#ffffff` (use paper `#f2f2ef`)  
- A second green, teal, or “AI purple”  
- Gradients, glow, drop shadow, or 3D bevel in the master files  
- Accent wordmark + accent mark on accent (no contrast)

The finished logo may be **drawn**, not typeset. If it stays type-based, keep these two families. Do not switch to Inter, Geist, or a serif.

---

## What to draw

**Prefer**

- A tight letter mark from **Z** and **x** that still reads at 16px  
- Geometry that can sit next to the Logic Core (icosahedron / node) without copying it  
- Square or near-square icon for favicon and app tile  
- Sharp corners, hairline construction — same chrome as the site  

**Avoid**

- Five orbiting nodes as the logo (that is the hero, not the mark)  
- Circuit traces, Wi‑Fi fans, robot heads, brain-in-chip clichés  
- A lotus / zen stone / meditation mark  
- Rounded bubbly app icons  
- A globe, atom, or DNA helix  

Meaning you can use, not illustrate: **one core, many surfaces** (web, mobile, agents, IoT, silicon).
